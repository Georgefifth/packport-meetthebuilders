# Demo storyboard

Limit: see project/config.json video_max_seconds. Aim 10 seconds under it.
At least 70% of the runtime must be `live` (real product footage). `slide` is for one architecture/proof frame at most.
Open on the problem or the result, not on a logo.

Rows must start with start and end seconds, then the type, so the gate can read them.

| start | end | type | on screen | narration (one idea per line, spoken plainly) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 8 | live | {{the painful situation or the final result}} | {{who hurts, in one sentence}} |
| 8 | 20 | live | {{landing page, one click on the example}} | {{what it does}} |
| 20 | 60 | live | {{core flow end to end}} | {{narrate the user's actions}} |
| 60 | 90 | live | {{the moment of value: the result and what it means}} | {{why this matters}} |
| 90 | 110 | live | {{second feature or edge case, brief}} | {{...}} |
| 110 | 130 | slide | {{one diagram: how it is built, and the sponsor tool(s) used}} | {{how we built it}} |
| 130 | 150 | live | {{close on the result}} | {{call to action and URL}} |
