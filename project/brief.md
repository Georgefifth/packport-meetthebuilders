# Brief

- One-sentence pitch: PackPort helps APAC food exporters catch customs label violations and generate compliant Singapore, Australia, and Japan export over-stickers without hiring expensive regulatory consultants.
- Primary user: An artisanal food or beverage founder in Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, or Malaysia preparing a product SKU for export to Singapore (SFA), Australia (FSANZ), or Japan (CAA).
- Problem: Small APAC food exporters face port detention and relabeling fines because allergen bolding, INS additive codes, per-100g nutrition tables, and health-claim limits differ across destination markets.
- Core flow: (1) Pick a preloaded APAC export SKU or paste your own label ingredients, nutrition facts, and claims plus a target destination market (Singapore SFA, Australia FSANZ, or Japan CAA) -> (2) Click "Run Customs Pre-Flight" to execute the deterministic regulatory threshold engine and Google Gemini (`@google/genai`) multi-agent inspection -> (3) Inspect the interactive package proof highlighting each violation by severity with exact statutory citations -> (4) Apply one-click regulatory fixes and export a print-ready compliant over-sticker (SVG/PNG) plus a 3-bullet customs clearance dossier.
- Instant demo path: Click **Try Sample: Bali Crispy Prawn Sambal → Singapore (SFA)** on the home screen (no login, no file upload needed) to see 4 flagged customs violations on the package proof and a print-ready compliant export over-sticker in under 5 seconds.
- Track: Path 02 — Hall of Fame Submissions ("Go for the Hall of Fame") & Path 01 — Get Featured Submission (aligned with Gen AI Academy APAC AI Agents & Data-Connected Tool Use).

## Out of scope
- User accounts, login walls, or multi-tenant organization billing management.
- Laboratory chemical spectroscopy or wet-lab nutritional assay calculation from raw recipes.
- Automated freight booking, customs brokerage EDI filing, or live ERP warehouse integration.
- Mobile native iOS/Android app wrapper (shipping as a responsive 1440px/390px web application).

## What a judge should remember
The customs pre-flight inspector that turned a non-compliant Indonesian sambal jar into a legally compliant, print-ready Singapore SFA export over-sticker in five seconds.
