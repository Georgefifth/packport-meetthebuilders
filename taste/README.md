# Taste calibration

The evaluator reads this folder before it scores a design. Put your own examples here, with a line on why.

- `good/`: seeded with Kiroween 2025 winners (see `good/README.md`). Add screenshots of products and hackathon entries you would be proud to ship. Next to each image add `name.md` with 2 to 4 lines: what works (layout, type, density, tone) and what to borrow.
- `bad/`: screenshots you hate, each with `name.md`: what is wrong in your words. Be specific ("the labels look like a control panel", "I could not tell what to click").

Aim for 5 of each. Add to it after every hackathon.

To measure how well the evaluator agrees with you, score 5 products blind (1 to 5 on design, clarity, originality), have the evaluator score the same screenshots, and compare. Large gaps mean the anchors in `.opencode/skills/browser-verification/references/scoring.md` need examples closer to your taste.

`bad/deskhud-os.md` is a worked example.
