# Reference winners: Kiroween 2025

Seeded 2026-11-24 from the hackathon's winners gallery and each project's Devpost page. Prizes were read from the project pages. Images are gallery screenshots the authors chose, kept here for private calibration. They belong to their authors. Do not publish this folder; the project template ignores image files in `taste/` so they never reach a public hackathon repo.

## What I could and could not check
- I looked at the gallery images of 13 entries. I did not open any live app, so nothing here describes how an app behaves.
- Prizes verified: InteractionKit (1st overall), UnitSet (2nd overall), SpookBnB (3rd overall), Faxi (Best Resurrection), rumorNet (Best Frankenstein), Seance (Best Costume Contest), Splat-n-Treat (Best Startup Project), plus bonus prizes for the rest.
- Not identified: the Skeleton Crew category winner. The gallery also shows Impulse Kit, Type To Death, Splice, Dark Ledger, Chimera, Sentinel, Haunted House Party and Lowkeygenius. My lookups either found no page or a page whose prize I could not confirm, so they are not included. Add them from the gallery if you want them.
- rumorNet has no image in this folder (its page exposed none I could download).

## What the placed entries share

1. **A hook a stranger can repeat.** "A rental site that turns into a horror escape room when you toggle dark mode." "Gesture-controlled games from one template." "Explore the world's darkest legends." "A design tool for Frankenstein-style UI."
2. **The theme or concept lives in the product**, not in a skin.
3. **A real identity: a mascot, an illustration language, or real photography.** Not gradients and not icon sets.
4. **Dark only where it is earned**, and then warm: charcoal or black with orange, not cyan. Light grounds for explanation.
5. **Legible text.** Even the dark ones use normal body sizes; the small-type examples are the weakest entries.
6. **One accent colour doing a job** (UnitSet's orange marks selection and primary actions).
7. **Process evidence in the gallery** (Seance, InteractionKit).
8. **Proof on real hardware or real use** (BlueKai, InteractionKit).
