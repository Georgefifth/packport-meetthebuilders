# What to ask of every design, from the winners

1. What is the one-sentence hook, and is it in the product (not only in the copy)?
2. Which single interaction or visual carries the theme?
3. Is the type legible at the size people will see it, with personality in the headline only?
4. Is there real imagery (photo, illustration, screenshot of real use), not decoration?
5. Does the gallery also show how the sponsor tool was used (specs, hooks, steering, agent logs)?
6. Would the grand-prize slide style work here: light ground, one message, big text, one picture?
